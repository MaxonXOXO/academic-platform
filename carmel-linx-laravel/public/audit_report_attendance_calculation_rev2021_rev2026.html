2. Detailed Audit: Revision 2021 (R21) System Logic (Updated)

Key Core Rules for Revision 2021:
1. Strictly Based on Actual Conducted & Attended: Attendance percentage is calculated strictly on actual sessions conducted and actual sessions attended ((actualAttended / actualConducted) × 100), never on proposed hours.
2. Direct Percentage-to-Marks Conversion: Raw attendance marks are computed as (percentage / 100) × maxAttendanceMarks.
3. Rounding Rule: Decimal values adjust to nearest: .5 and above → next whole/half number; below .5 → lower number.
4. Exam Eligibility Isolated to Tutor Report: Exam eligibility rules are applicable exclusively to the consolidated attendance of all subjects together in the Tutor Report. All individual subject evaluation and marks registers strictly avoid attendance shortage/eligibility status notes.

5. Critical Audit Observations for Academic Review

1. Discrepancy in R21 Between Theory and Other Courses:
In Revision 2021, students in Theory receive 0 Marks if their attendance falls below 75%. However, in Drawing, Seminar, and Major Project, students between 65% and 75% receive partial attendance marks (20% to 40% of the maximum weight). This was by SBTE R21 design, but created confusion among staff.
2. R21 Lab Attendance Linear vs Slabs:
R21 Practical calculates attendance as a direct linear fraction: (Present / Total) × 15. A student with 89% receives 13.4M, whereas in R21 Theory an 89% student falls into the 9M stepdown slab.
3. Complete Unification in Revision 2026 (R26):
Revision 2026 completely resolves prior discrepancies. Every subject type strictly implements Table 2.1 (Max 5 Marks). Outcome attainment calculations also correctly isolate academic competencies by subtracting attendance marks where required.
