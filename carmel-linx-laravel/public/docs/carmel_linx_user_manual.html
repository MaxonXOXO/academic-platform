Chapter 1: Platform Architecture, Organization Setup & Onboarding Hierarchy

1.1 SBTE Keralam Polytechnic Operating Framework

Carmel-linx (clinx) is an automated, outcome-based academic management platform engineered specifically for the State Board of Technical Education (SBTE) Keralam polytechnic system. It provides an intelligent, networked academic ecosystem that interconnects administration, departmental leadership, instructional faculty, students, and parents into unified, role-specific digital workspaces.

Integrated with in-house Agentic AI solutions (led by Carmie, your virtual campus mentor), Carmel-linx eliminates operational bottlenecks, automates continuous assessment scaling, manages course files, and provides real-life step-by-step guidance across desktop and mobile devices.

1.2 Organizational Setup Flow (Superadmin Level)

Every polytechnic organization begins at the Superadmin level. The setup process is strictly sequential:

Phase Action Items Operational Impact
Phase 1: Institution Details Configure Polytechnic Name, Institutional Code, Affiliation details (SBTE Kerala & DTE), Campus contact information. Establishes the central institutional entity and letterhead templates for all official certificates and NBA reports.
Phase 2: Programs & Branches Create Diploma engineering branches (e.g. Civil, Mechanical, Electrical, Electronics, Computer, Automobile). Enables department isolation and initializes departmental academic registries.
Phase 3: Principal Assignment Provision and assign the executive Principal user account. Transfers operational administrative authority to the Principal for day-to-day college management.

1.3 HOD Onboarding & Principal Approval Workflow

Following institution initialization, the Principal onboards all Heads of Departments (HODs) into the platform. This flow is illustrated below:

Key Rules for HOD Registration:

  • Registration Medium: HODs can register using their mobile phone via the mobile app version or through the desktop web browser.
  • Staff Registration ID: The HOD's Staff Registration ID must be their working Phone Number.
  • Mandatory Working Email ID: An active personal/institutional email address is mandatory for two-factor recovery, system alerts, and password resets.
  • Password: HODs choose their own secure personal password.
  • Principal Approval Gate: An HOD account remains Inactive / Pending immediately after registration. It requires explicit approval and authorization from the Principal before the HOD can log in.
  • Departmental Authority: Upon Principal authorization, the HOD gains full administrative power over their department (staff workload assignments, batch creation, syllabus registry, and student rosters).

Carmel-linx is engineered as a Virtual Institution with Virtual Classrooms operating two concurrent syllabus frameworks with strict digital isolation:

Chapter 2: Head of Department (HOD) Operations Manual

The Head of Department (HOD) Console (/dashboard/hod) serves as the departmental command post. Once authorized by the Principal, the HOD exercises complete administrative governance over departmental staff, academic batches, curriculum allocations, timetable scheduling, and faculty workloads.

2.2 User Directory & Staff Approval Operations

The HOD's first operational duty immediately following login is staff verification and activation:

  • Access User Directory: Click User Directory in the HOD left sidebar. This displays an interactive master table of all registered faculty, technical staff, and students belonging to the department.
  • Mandatory Staff Approval: Locate newly registered staff members in the table and click Approve. Once approved, the staff member is immediately cleared to log in via mobile app or desktop to access their teaching dashboard.
  • Directory Management Options: The table allows inline editing of staff profiles, resetting forgotten user passwords, and deleting erroneous or retired accounts.

2.3 Batch Creation & Tutor/Mentor Allocation

For each academic cycle and admission year, a new class batch must be initialized:

  1. Navigate to Batch Management or locate the Create Batch button at the top-left of the HOD panel.
  2. Select the target Admission Year (e.g. Admission 2026 for newly admitted Semester 1 students).
  3. Assign the official Class Tutor and Class Mentor from the department faculty dropdowns.
  4. Confirm creation. A newly formed Class / Batch Card (e.g. Admission 2026 - Semester 1) appears on the HOD dashboard, ready for curriculum mapping.

2.4 Subject Allocation & Multi-Staff Mapping Workflow

Curriculum subjects must be mapped to the newly formed class:

  1. Click Subject Allocation in the HOD left sidebar.
  2. Select the Target Batch and Semester from the header dropdowns.
  3. Click Add Subject to launch the Add Curriculum Subject modal.
  4. Specify the Subject Code, select Subject Type (Theory, Practical / Lab, Practicum, Drawing, Seminar), enter the Subject Name, and select the Syllabus Revision (REV2021 or REV2026).
  5. Click Add Subject to record the course in the batch's curriculum matrix.

Crucial Teaching Staff Allocation Rules:

Subject Type Staff Assignment Rule Operational Impact
Theory Subjects Assign only the primary designated Lecturer. Grants exclusive theory classroom control, attendance logging, and series marks entry to that lecturer.
Lab & Practical Subjects Assign all involved faculty and technical staff (Lecturer, Demonstrator, Workshop Instructor, Tradesman). Shares the virtual laboratory among all instructors for simultaneous experiment rubric evaluations and logbook tracking.
Inter-Departmental Courses Select the concerned department from the dropdown (e.g. Basic Science, Humanities, Central Workshop) and check the assigned faculty member. Enables visiting faculty from other departments to access this classroom and have their teaching hours reflected in inter-departmental workload reports.

2.5 The "Manage Batch" Console (The 5 Configuration Tabs)

Clicking the Manage Batch button on any batch card opens the primary administrative console containing 5 specialized tabs:

  • Tab 1: Tutor & Mentor: Displays the current academic advisors. HOD can reassign or reset the Tutor and Mentor if departmental duties shift.
  • Tab 2: Allocated Subjects: Lists all curriculum subjects mapped to the batch alongside their assigned lecturers, demonstrators, and instructors.
  • Tab 3: Enrolled Students: Displays the verified student roster for the batch once student registration and HOD/Tutor approvals are complete.
  • Tab 4: Time Table: The master weekly period scheduling grid (see Section 2.6 for timetable rules).
  • Tab 5: Semester History: Archive displaying academic progression, CIE reports, and results from previous semesters (blank for new S1 batches).

2.6 Master Timetable, Split Labs & Automated Workload Balancing

The weekly timetable configured in Tab 4 serves as the automated engine for the institutional Staff Workload Report:

  • Timetable Staff Inclusions: When scheduling periods, the HOD must include only the lecturer for theory slots, but must include all assigned staff (Lecturers, Demonstrators, Instructors) for lab slots, and include lab staff for practicum slots. This directly drives workload accounting across departments.
  • Split Batch Management for Labs: If a class is split into two halves (Batch A & Batch B) attending two different practical labs simultaneously in the same period, Carmel-linx supports concurrent dual-lab scheduling in that time slot.
  • Automatic Practicum Workload Balancing: For courses designated as Practicum (combined theory and practical), Carmel-linx automatically balances faculty workload:
    • If scheduled as a multi-hour block (e.g. 2 or 3 continuous hours), the period is automatically calculated as Lab workload.
    • If scheduled as a single 1-hour slot, it is calculated as Theory workload.

2.7 Student Credential Printing & Destructive Batch Deletion Policy

Two critical action buttons exist on the Manage Batch card:

  • "Print Credentials" Button (Top of Card): Used when a batch of students is bulk-uploaded into the platform by the Tutor or HOD rather than individual student self-registration. Clicking this generates a printable credential slipsheet containing temporary login IDs and default passwords for physical distribution. Students must change their password upon their first login, and their permanent ID updates to their SBTE / admission number.
  • "Delete Batch" Button (Bottom of Card): A high-risk destructive control that prompts for explicit confirmation.
    CRITICAL WARNING: Confirming batch deletion permanently erases all batch records, student enrollments, subject mappings, timetable schedules, and continuous internal assessment (CIE) data forever. It cannot be recovered. This action is strictly reserved for correcting accidental batch creation errors during initial setup. Never delete an active batch.

2.8 HOD Instructional Role: "My Batches" Virtual Classrooms

If the Head of Department also handles teaching responsibilities, Carmel-linx eliminates the need to switch user accounts or log in under another role:

  • Access "My Batches": Click the My Batches link in the HOD left sidebar.
  • Dual-Role Capability: The HOD seamlessly transitions into an instructional faculty / lecturer persona for their assigned courses.
  • Virtual Classroom Delivery: Clicking any allotted course card opens the specialized virtual classroom (Theory, Lab, Practicum, Drawing, etc.) according to the syllabus revision (R2021 or R2026).
  • Documentation & Grading: HODs can log daily hourly attendance, record formative assignments, manage series examinations, and compile course files directly from this view.

Chapter 3: Class Tutor & Mentor Operations Manual

3.1 Role Overview, Mission & Sequential Positioning

In the SBTE Keralam polytechnic structure, the Class Tutor is a specially assigned instructional faculty member appointed by the Head of Department (HOD) to shepherd an entire class batch through their academic lifecycle. The Class Tutor acts as the central administrative and pastoral linchpin of the class.

Crucial Sequential Prerequisite: The Tutor's onboarding and roster finalization workflow sits directly between HOD Batch Creation and Lecturer Virtual Classroom Operations. Subject teachers, demonstrators, and trade instructors cannot effectively conduct virtual classrooms, record continuous assessments, or track attendance until the Class Tutor verifies student registrations, assigns roll numbers, and establishes the active class roll.

3.3 Supervised Class Roster & Student Account Management

From the Tutor Dashboard (/dashboard/tutor), navigate to My Batches → Class Roster. This interactive administrative table provides full-lifecycle supervision over every student enrolled in the batch:

Roster Control Action & Trigger Institutional Rule & Governance

Suspend Student Toggle account status to Suspended. Freezes student login and submissions during disciplinary proceedings without destroying historical marks or attendance.

3.4 Flexible Roll Number Assignment

Roll numbers govern all classroom registers, practical lab experiment logs, exam seating plans, and attendance reporting. The Tutor Console provides flexible roll number sequencing:

  • Automated Alphabetical Ordering: Automatically re-indexes student roll numbers from 1 to N ordered alphabetically by student full name.
  • Admission Number Sequencing: Assigns roll numbers based on official polytechnic admission serial order.
  • Custom Manual Ordering: Drag-and-drop or direct numerical entry allowing tutors to handle exceptional cases (e.g., late lateral entry admissions or readmitted students) without disturbing existing indices.
  • Instant Roster Sync: Once confirmed, roll numbers instantly propagate across all virtual classrooms, teacher attendance registers, and marksheets.

3.5 50/50 Dual Mentoring System & Parent SMS Activation

To ensure deep student care and holistic progress monitoring under SBTE polytechnic guidelines, each class batch is split evenly between two designated mentors:

  • 50% Primary Mentorship (Class Tutor): The Class Tutor directly mentors the first half of the class roster (Roll 1 to N/2).
  • 50% Secondary Mentorship (Assigned Mentor): The HOD-allocated Second Mentor mentors the remaining half of the class roster (Roll (N/2)+1 to N).
  • Digital Mentoring Diary: An interlinked pastoral log where:
    • Students Record: SWOT analysis, personal interests, family background, academic difficulties, career goals, and co-curricular achievements.
    • Mentors Record: Date-stamped one-on-one counseling notes, academic observation summaries, behavioral interventions, and parent consultation logs.
  • Parent Onboarding & Automated SMS: The Tutor triggers the Send Parent Welcome SMS action. Carmel-linx dispatches personalized SMS notifications containing unique, passwordless direct web links. Parents gain instant, 24/7 mobile access to their ward's live attendance percentage, continuous marks, and tutor remarks without complicated registration steps.

3.6 Consolidated Attendance, Low Attendance Tracking & SBTE Condonation

While subject teachers log session-by-session attendance, the Class Tutor oversees the holistic attendance health of the batch:

  • Live Master Attendance Grid: Consolidates hour-by-hour records across Theory, Practical, Practicum, Drawing, and Workshop subjects into a single comprehensive percentage meter.
  • Automated Low Attendance Alerts (< 75%): Any student falling below the mandatory SBTE 75% threshold is automatically highlighted with red visual indicators and queued for parental SMS notification.
  • SBTE Medical Condonation Processing (65% – 74%):
    • Students with attendance between 65.0% and 74.9% qualify for condonation consideration on medical grounds or authorized institutional duty.
    • The Tutor Console compiles the official SBTE Condonation Report, attaches verified medical certificates, logs reason codes, and submits the dossier to the Principal for formal Board sanction.
  • Individual Attendance Slips: Tutor can generate and print individual attendance summaries and warnings prior to Series Exams and End-Semester Examinations.

3.7 Parent-Teacher Association (PTA) Progress Reports

Prior to class PTA conferences, the Tutor Console provides a one-click compilation tool to generate official Student PTA Progress Cards:

  • Unified Academic Breakdown: Aggregates Course Outcome (CO) attainment, Series Exam 1 & 2 marks, formative assignment grades, and laboratory continuous evaluation scores across all enrolled courses.
  • Attendance Summary: Displays total working hours, hours attended, and percentage with condonation flags.
  • Tutor Remarks & Signature Blocks: Pre-formats qualitative mentor observations, strengths, areas for improvement, and formal signature lines for Tutor, HOD, and Parent.
  • Export & Print Options: High-resolution PDF batch download formatted for standard A4 printing.

3.8 Digital Approvals: Student Leave & SBTE 75-Point Activity Certificates

The Tutor Console serves as the primary verification checkpoint for external student submissions:

  • Digital Leave Applications: Students submit leave requests through their portal specifying dates, duration, medical/personal grounds, and documentary proof. The Tutor reviews, adds remarks, and issues digital approvals that feed into condonation tracking.
  • SBTE Activity Points (75-Point Requirement): SBTE Keralam mandates 75 activity points for Diploma graduation (earned through NSS, NCC, Sports, Arts, Paper Presentations, Hackathons, Professional Bodies, Community Service, and MOOCs).
    • Students upload certificates and activity details via their portal.
    • The Class Tutor inspects uploaded certificate proofs, validates point eligibility against the SBTE Activity Point Guidebook, and sanctions verified points into the student's graduation ledger.

Chapter 4: Lecturer / Academic Staff Operations Manual

4.1 Overview & Dashboard Operational Entry Point

The Lecturer Console manages teaching delivery, virtual classroom sessions, syllabus parsing, lesson planning, assignments, continuous internal evaluation (CIA/CIE), series examinations, student surveys, and CO-PO attainment.

Operational Access: Navigate to /dashboard/lecturer via sidebar or main menu. If logged in as a Tutor, HOD, or Principal, access your instructional subjects from the "My Batches" sidebar link.

Upon login, the console presents the Allotted Batch Cards. Each card displays the department, semester, class batch, assigned subjects, and enrolled student count. Clicking any subject card automatically launches the appropriate virtual classroom based on the batch admission year and subject type:

  • Batches Prior to 2026 Admission (Admissions 2021–2025): Governed by Revision 2021 (R-2021) rules and evaluation schemes. Clicking a Theory subject card opens the Virtual Theory Classroom R-2021.
  • Batches from 2026 Admission Onwards (Admission 2026+): Governed by Revision 2026 (R-2026) criteria (Theory, Practicum Basic Science, Practicum Program Core, Lab, Drawing, Physical Education).

4.2.1 Step 1: SITTTR Syllabus PDF Download & Automated Extraction

The foundational step in initializing an R-2021 theory virtual classroom is uploading the official curriculum syllabus:

  1. Download the official course syllabus PDF from the SITTTR website (State Institute of Technical Teachers' Training and Research, Kerala - sitttr.ac.in).
  2. In the virtual classroom, click the blue "Upload Syllabus PDF" button.
  3. Carmel-linx's syllabus parser automatically extracts the Course Outcomes (CO1, CO2, CO3, CO4), the Bloom's Taxonomy cognitive mappings, the module syllabus breakdown, and reference textbooks.

4.2.2 Tab 1: Course Structure & Academic Metadata Confirmation

The Course Structure tab provides full visibility into the parsed curriculum framework:

  • Academic Metadata Banner: Positioned immediately below the subject title card, this prominent status bar confirms:
    • Batch: [e.g. CE_2024_2027] • Sem: [e.g. S4] • Branch: [e.g. CE]
    • Revision: R-2021 • Proposed Hours: 45 or 60 hrs • Credits: 3.0 / 4.0
    • CIA: 50M • ESE: 100M / 75M
  • Review & Confirm: The instructor reviews the Course Outcomes, Module topics, and CO-PO Articulation Matrix. Inline correction controls allow instructors to refine any text prior to proceeding.

4.2.3 Tab 2: Lesson Planner & "Sync Dates from Log Data"

The Lesson Planner tab provides a structured, hour-by-hour roadmap of instructional delivery:

  • 1-Hour Session Divisions: Each row corresponds to a standard 1-hour instructional period, pre-populated with module topics and CO mapping.
  • 60-Hour Course Framework (~62 Total Sessions): For a standard 60-hour theory course, the planner generates approximately 62 sessions—accounting for 60 lecture/tutorial hours plus 2 to 4 dedicated one-hour sessions for internal series examinations and continuous evaluation.
  • Inline Editing & Row Customization: Instructors can freely edit topic descriptions, delete rows, or click "Add Row" to insert custom tutorial or revision sessions.
  • Proposed Dates & Autosave: Set target calendar dates for each session. Changes are safeguarded with live autosave alongside a manual "Save Lesson Plan" button.
  • "Sync Dates from Log Data" (Automated Execution Dates): Rather than retyping completion dates, instructors simply click the "Sync Dates from Log Data" button. Carmel-linx queries recorded class attendance logs (entered daily or imported in bulk from MS Teams PDFs) and automatically fills the actual completion dates into the lesson plan matching the exact timetable dates taught.

4.2.4 Tab 3: Formative Assessment (Assignments, Lock & Amber Submission Tracking)

In the SBTE Revision 2021 curriculum, continuous evaluation follows a strict statutory formula:

Evaluation Component Percentage Weight Allotted Marks Assessment Mechanism
Summative Assessment 40% 20 Marks Series Examinations / Written Tests
Formative Assessment 40% 20 Marks 4 CO-Wise Assignments (Consolidated)
Attendance 20% 10 Marks Automated Attendance % Scale
Total Continuous Internal Assessment (CIA) 100% 50 Marks Sum: 20 + 20 + 10 = 50 Marks

Formative Assessment Operational Workflow:

  • 4 CO-Wise Assignments: The 20 formative marks are assessed across 4 separate assignments—one dedicated to each Course Outcome (CO1, CO2, CO3, and CO4).
  • "Generate Questions" Engine:
    • AI Generation (Gemini): Automatically generates syllabus-aligned assignment questions based on Bloom's Cognitive Levels (Remember, Understand, Apply, Analyze).
    • Manual Entry: Compose custom questions with rich formatting.
    • Pull from Question Bank: Retrieve previously saved questions from the departmental repository.
    • Image / Diagram Insertion: Attach circuit schematics, engineering drawings, or formula figures directly into question statements.
    • Start & Due Dates: Set submission deadlines and print official question papers.
  • LOCK Button & Student To-Do List Dispatch:
    • Once questions, rubrics, and deadlines are finalized, the instructor clicks the LOCK icon on the CO card.
    • Locking freezes questions against accidental modification and instantly dispatches automated notifications to all enrolled students. The assignment, questions, and due date automatically appear in each student's portal To-Do List.

4.7 Universal Course & Program Exit Survey and Attainment Calculation

Carmel-Linx features a unified Outcome-Based Education (OBE) Attainment Engine integrated universally across all virtual classrooms (Theory, Practical / Lab, Seminar, Major Project, and Drawing).

4.7.1 Direct vs. Indirect Attainment Pipeline

In alignment with National Board of Accreditation (NBA) Criterion 3 and SBTE OBE frameworks, overall Course Outcome (CO) attainment is calculated through a weighted combination of direct academic performance and student indirect feedback:

Overall CO Attainment = (80% × Direct Attainment) + (20% × Indirect Survey Attainment)
  • Direct Attainment (80% Weightage):
    • Calculated from continuous assessments (Assignments, Series Exams, Class Quizzes, Lab Rubrics, Seminar Presentations, Project Milestones) and End Semester Examinations (ESE).
    • Carmel-Linx tracks attainment levels based on target benchmarks: Level 3 (≥ 70% students attain benchmark), Level 2 (≥ 60% students attain benchmark), Level 1 (≥ 50% students attain benchmark).
  • Indirect Attainment (20% Weightage - Online Student Exit Surveys):
    • Course Exit Survey: Administered online to enrolled students at the conclusion of each course. Students rate their attainment of each specific Course Outcome (CO1 through CO5) on a 1–5 or 1–3 Likert scale.
    • Program Exit Survey: Conducted by the Class Tutor or HOD for graduating batches to evaluate overall Program Outcomes (PO1–PO11) and Program Specific Outcomes (PSO1–PSO3).

4.7.2 Role of HOD and Class Tutor in Attainment Generation

  • Triggering the Online Survey: The Class Tutor or HOD activates the digital exit survey from their console with one click. Enrolled students receive instant notifications on their Student Portal dashboard.
  • Automated Aggregation: As students submit their surveys online, Carmel-Linx compiles survey response averages in real time.
  • Generation of Program Attainment & NBA Statements: Once surveys are locked, the system automatically computes:
    • Course CO-PO Mapping Attainment Table.
    • Consolidated Department Program Attainment Matrix (aggregating Theory, Lab, Seminar, Drawing, and Project attainment across all semesters).
    • NBA Criterion 3 Compliance Dossier with graphical spider/radar charts and action plans for continuous improvement.
