Virtual Lab Operations — Complete Staff Manual & Step-by-Step Guide

Curriculum Compliance: SBTE Revision 2021 (75 Marks CIA) • All Dates Standardized in DD-MM-YYYY

1. Revision 2021 Marks Structure & Assessment Split-up (75M Total CIA)

Assessment Component Component Max CIA Weight Evaluation Rules & Rubrics
Continuous Lab Work 37.5 Marks 37.5 Marks Average of all conducted experiments evaluated across 5 rubrics (Max 37.5M)
Open-Ended Experiment 7.5 Marks 7.5 Marks Micro-project / individual problem-solving challenge evaluated out of 7.5 Marks
Practical Series Tests Test 1: 15M
Test 2: 15M		 15.0 Marks Average of Practical Test 1 & Test 2: (Test 1 + Test 2) / 2 (Max 15M)
Lab Attendance 100% Scale 15.0 Marks Proportional: (Attendance % / 100) × 15 (e.g. 100% → 15M, 90% → 13.5M, 80% → 12M, 75% → 11.3M)
TOTAL CONTINUOUS INTERNAL ASSESSMENT (CIA) 75 MARKS 37.5 (Lab Work) + 7.5 (Open-Ended) + 15 (Tests) + 15 (Attendance) = 75M
📋 5 Rubrics for Continuous Lab Work (37.5 Marks Total per Experiment):
1. Rough Record / Algorithm: 0 – 5.0 Marks  |  2. Fair Record / Documentation: 0 – 7.5 Marks
3. Observation / Preparation: 0 – 7.5 Marks  |  4. Execution / Procedure & Punctuality: 0 – 7.5 Marks  |  5. Viva Voce / Viva Understanding: 0 – 10.0 Marks
Total per experiment = 5 + 7.5 + 7.5 + 7.5 + 10 = 37.5 Marks

3. Step-by-Step Operations for Assigned Lab Staff (From Scratch)

Step 1: Login & Navigation

  • Desktop: Log in with faculty credentials → Lecturer Dashboard → Click your assigned Practical Course card (e.g. 3049 - Fundamentals of C Programming Lab).
  • Mobile: Log in → Tap Virtual Lab in bottom menu → Select your Subject.

Step 2: Upload Syllabus & Course Structure Setup

  • Open the Syllabus / Course Structure tab.
  • Upload the official SBTE Revision 2021 syllabus PDF/Word document or import from template.
  • Verify course metadata: Subject Name, Subject Code, Semester, Department, Lecture/Lab Hours, and Credits.

Step 3: CO - PO - PSO Setup & Mapping Matrix

  • Open CO-PO Mapping tab.
  • Set up Course Outcomes (CO1, CO2, CO3, etc.) with Bloom's Taxonomy cognitive levels and practical competencies.
  • Define correlation levels with Program Outcomes (PO1 to PO7) and Program Specific Outcomes (PSOs):
    3 = Substantial (High), 2 = Moderate (Medium), 1 = Slight (Low), - = No Correlation.

Step 4: Lesson Plan Setup & Schedule

  • Open the Lesson Plan / Planner tab.
  • Configure day-wise practical session entries: Day No, Proposed Date (DD-MM-YYYY), Topic/Experiment description, mapped CO Tag, Pedagogy, and Actual Conducted Date.
  • Auto-saves as you enter or import from the department template.

Step 5: Formative Tab (Continuous Lab Work & Experiment List)

  • Experiment List Setup: Verify all syllabus experiments (Exp 1 to Exp 20) with titles and mapped CO tags.
  • Grading Students with 5 Rubric Sliders:
    • On Desktop: Open Continuous Evaluation → Select Exp No → Click student row → Adjust the 5 compact horizontal sliders (Rough 5M, Fair 7.5M, Obs 7.5M, Proc 7.5M, Viva 10M) arranged neatly on a single row → Live total (Max 37.5M) updates → Click Save / Next.
    • On Mobile: Tap student card → Adjust touch sliders → Instant auto-save.
  • Open-Ended Experiment (7.5M): Enter assigned micro-project topic & evaluated score out of 7.5 Marks.

Step 6: Summative Tab (Practical Series Tests & Question Bank)

  • Question Bank Preparation: Add practical questions tagged with COs and max marks into the Course Question Bank.
  • Test Question Setup: Pick questions from the Question Bank or generate structured question papers for Practical Test 1 & Practical Test 2.
  • Test Mark Entry (15M Total): Enter exam dates (DD-MM-YYYY) → Enter Test 1 (Max 15M) and Test 2 (Max 15M). System automatically computes the average: (Test 1 + Test 2) / 2 (Max 15M).

Step 7: Daily Attendance & Class Logging (Period Presets, Dates & Batches)

  • Open Attendance & Class Log. Select session date (DD-MM-YYYY).
  • Select continuous period preset: P1–P3 Morning (3h) or P4–P6 Afternoon (3h).
  • Select the batch: Batch 1, Batch 2, or Whole Class.
  • Select syllabus experiment from dropdown to auto-link hours and CO tags.
  • Click Mark All Present (uncheck absentees or tap grid buttons) → Click Save Class Log & Attendance.

Step 8: Log Management & Debugging (Edit / Delete / Date Adjustments)

  • Delete Accidental Duplicate Logs: Open Past Logs → Look for amber Duplicate badge → Click Delete. The duplicate is deleted safely while preserving original logs and attendance.
  • Edit Experiment Conducted Date: Open Completed Experiments modal → Click date picker or Tune icon → Set corrected DD-MM-YYYY date → Select scope (Universal or Batch-specific) → Click Save & Update.

Step 9: Proportional Attendance Calculation & Live 75M Master Sheet

  • System calculates attendance marks proportionately: (Present Classes / Total Classes) × 15.
  • Open Consolidated CIA (75M) to review the master evaluation sheet:
    37.5M (Lab Work) + 7.5M (Open-Ended) + 15M (Series Tests) + 15M (Attendance) = 75.0M TOTAL CIA.
  • Faculty can review and fine-tune attendance marks (0–15) in this sheet if institutional duty leaves or approved condonation apply.

4. OBE Attainment Workflow: Surveys, End-Sem Grades & Thresholds

1. End-Semester Course Exit Survey (Indirect Attainment - 20%):

Students log in to their student portal and complete the Course Exit Survey rating their competence for each Course Outcome (CO) on a 3-point scale:

  • 3 (High / Substantial)  |  2 (Moderate)  |  1 (Slight / Low)

System computes average indirect attainment rating on a 3.0 scale: Indirect Level = (Average Score / 3) × 3.0.

2. Threshold Configuration by Faculty (Before Calculation):

  • Internal CIA Mark Threshold: e.g. Score ≥ 60% or 70% of marks in CO-mapped components.
  • End-Semester Examination (ESE) Grade Threshold: e.g. Achieving Grade A or B (or ≥ 50% / 60% mark equivalent).
  • Student Cohort Target %: Percentage of students achieving threshold to earn Level 3, 2, or 1.

3. End-Semester Exam (ESE) Attainment (Uniform Across All COs):

Because SBTE publishes semester exam results as overall course Grades (S, A, B, C, D, F) rather than individual CO question marks, the End-Semester Exam attainment is uniform across all Course Outcomes (COs) in that course.

Example ESE Attainment (Target: Grade A):

  • Level 3 (High): ≥ 70% of total students achieve Grade A or above.
  • Level 2 (Medium): 60% – 69% of students achieve Grade A or above.
  • Level 1 (Low): 50% – 59% of students achieve Grade A or above.
  • Level 0 (Unattained): < 50% of students achieve Grade A or above.

4. Internal CIA Direct Attainment (Per CO):

Computed for each specific CO from its mapped Continuous Lab Work (37.5M), Open-Ended (7.5M), and Series Tests (15M):

  • If ≥ 70% (or configured target, e.g. 65%) of students score ≥ 60% marks in CO1 tasks → CO1 Internal Level = 3.

5. Final Overall CO Attainment Formula (100% Total):

Attainment Component Weightage Calculation & Data Source
Direct Attainment 80% (50% × Internal CIA Level) + (50% × End-Sem Grade Level)
Indirect Attainment 20% Student End-Semester Course Exit Survey average rating (Scale 1 to 3).
FINAL OVERALL CO ATTAINMENT 100% (0.80 × Direct Attainment) + (0.20 × Indirect Attainment) → Mapped to NBA CO-PO-PSO Matrix

✅ DOs (Best Practices)

  • Log continuous 3-hour sessions using multi-period selector (e.g. P1–P3).
  • Link syllabus experiment from dropdown to maintain CO tags.
  • Grade rubric sliders while observing student in lab.
  • Configure attainment thresholds prior to final NBA report export.

❌ DON'Ts

  • Do not create separate 1-hour logs for a continuous 3-hour lab.
  • Do not delete student rosters manually (handled by Tutor).
  • Do not leave experiments unlinked to Course Outcomes.
  • Do not print registers without verifying DD-MM-YYYY dates.
